<div class="section-header">Key Accomplishments</div>

<div class="accomplishment-item">
<p>Engineered a political stance classifier using RAG and hybrid NLP-graph techniques, achieving <strong>90%</strong> accuracy by <strong>fine-tuning</strong> large language models and designing prompt templates to improve generalization across domains.</p>
</div>

<div class="accomplishment-item">
<p>Constructed scalable embedding pipelines with <strong>LangChain</strong>, <strong>pgvector</strong>, and <strong>spaCy-based NLP preprocessing</strong>, aggregating over <strong>70,000+</strong> social media textual records (Twitter, Reddit) to enhance training diversity and robustness.</p>
</div>

<div class="accomplishment-item">
<p>Benchmarked open-source and proprietary LLMs <strong>(Claude, Gemini, Mistral, LLaMA)</strong> across <strong>10+</strong> political topics, applying cross-model bias mitigation and visualizing results in <strong>Tableau</strong> to assess fairness.</p>
</div>
